How Gifts Work

Gifts are purchased from the Gift Shop. You can open them to get random items – toys, vehicles, strollers, accessories, and sometimes pets. Different gift tiers have different drop rates.

What You Can Get from Gifts

Small Gift (100 Bucks)

  • Common: Food items, basic toys (plushies, balls).
  • Uncommon: Basic accessories (hats, glasses).
  • Rare: Rare toys, small strollers.

Medium Gift (300 Bucks)

  • Rare: Accessories, vehicles, strollers.
  • Ultra-Rare: Legendary toys (dragon plush, unicorn plush).
  • Legendary: Very rare – sometimes pets.

Large Gift (500 Bucks)

  • Ultra-Rare: Rare vehicles, strollers.
  • Legendary: Legendary toys, rare accessories.
  • Mythical: Rare chance for a legendary pet.

Special Gift (1,200 Bucks)

  • Guaranteed legendary item – could be a toy, vehicle, stroller, or pet.
  • Best chance: Has the highest chance for a legendary pet.

Best Gifts to Buy

  • Best value: Medium Gift (300 Bucks) – good balance of price and rewards.
  • Best chance for legendaries: Special Gift (1,200 Bucks) – guaranteed legendary item.
  • Best for beginners: Small Gift (100 Bucks) – cheap and fun.
  • Avoid: Large Gift (500 Bucks) – overpriced for the odds.

Gift Opening Strategy

  • Save up for Special Gifts – they're the only way to guarantee legendary drops.
  • Open gifts in bulk – some players believe it increases legendary chance.
  • Buy gifts during events – gift items are sometimes event-exclusive.
  • Don't waste money on Small Gifts – they rarely give good items.

Pro Tips

  • Gifts are fun but not profitable – expect to lose value most of the time.
  • If you're F2P, avoid gifts – save bucks for eggs and houses.
  • If you have extra bucks, buy Special Gifts – they're the best chance for legendary pets.
  • Trade your gift items – some items are worth more than the gift itself.
