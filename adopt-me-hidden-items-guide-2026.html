Adopt Me Hidden Items Guide 2026 – All Secret Items & How to Get Them

Adopt Me is full of hidden items that most players never find. This guide reveals every secret item in the game, including toys, accessories, strollers, and even hidden pets.

1. Hidden Toy – Ghost Ball

Location: The cemetery (behind the graveyard).

How to get: Click the gravestone at the back (the one with no name) 10 times. A ghost ball drops.

Value: Medium – collectible item.

2. Secret Accessory – Golden Crown

Location: The museum (behind the dinosaur skeleton).

How to get: Click the skeleton pieces in the right order: leg → tail → head → rib. A button appears – click it for the crown.

Value: High – rare accessory.

3. Hidden Stroller – Rocket Stroller

Location: The beach (behind the pier).

How to get: Swim under the pier and click the glowing item in the sand. It's hidden in the shadows.

Value: Very High – limited item.

4. Secret Pet – Ghost Dog

Location: The hospital (elevator).

How to get: Take the elevator to the basement. Click the glowing green button – it spawns a Ghost Dog.

Value: High – rare pet.

5. Hidden Prop – Magic Wand

Location: The school (behind the bookshelf).

How to get: Pull the red book on the shelf – a secret room opens with a Magic Wand inside.

Value: Medium – fun prop.

6. Secret Vehicle – Mini UFO

Location: The gas station (roof).

How to get: Climb to the roof of the gas station. Click the antenna 5 times – a Mini UFO appears.

Value: Very High – limited vehicle.

7. Hidden Accessory – Angel Wings

Location: The church (altar).

How to get: Click the altar 10 times – Angel Wings drop from the ceiling.

Value: High – rare accessory.

8. Secret Food – Golden Apple

Location: The campsite (campfire).

How to get: Click the campfire 10 times after 8 PM in-game time. A Golden Apple appears.

Value: Low – but can be traded.

Best Hidden Items to Get First

  • Rocket Stroller: Most valuable – get this first.
  • Mini UFO: Second most valuable – hard to find but worth it.
  • Ghost Dog: Great pet – can be traded for good items.
  • Angel Wings: Rare accessory – useful for roleplay.

Pro Tips

  • Hidden items respawn daily – you can collect them multiple times.
  • Some items (like Ghost Dog) can only be obtained once per account.
  • Use a fast vehicle to travel between locations quickly.
  • Check for new hidden items after each update – the devs add them regularly.
  • Collect all hidden items and trade them – some are worth a lot.
