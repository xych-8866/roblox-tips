Combo Basics

A good combo has three parts: Starter → Extender → Finisher. Timing is everything – practice each combo in training mode before using it in ranked matches.

Godhuman Combos

Combo 1: One-Shot Godhuman + Kitsune

  1. C (Kitsune) – dash in, stun
  2. Z (Kitsune) – extra damage
  3. Godhuman C – uppercut launch
  4. X (Kitsune) – follow-up damage
  5. Godhuman Z – ground slam
  6. Godhuman X – finisher

Total Damage: ~90% HP

Combo Practice Tips

  • Practice in training mode – no pressure, just timing.
  • Start with one combo – master it before learning another.
  • Watch your cooldowns – don't spam moves.
  • Use a private server – practice against friends.
  • Watch pro players – see how they chain moves together.

Pro Tips

  • Timing matters – wait for the animation to finish before pressing the next move.
  • Use mobility moves first – they create space and set up combos.
  • Adapt to your opponent – don't use the same combo every time.
  • Stay calm – if you miss a combo, don't panic.
