How Skills Work

Each job has its own skill tree. You earn skill points by doing job tasks. Each point unlocks a new ability or bonus for that job.

How to Earn Skill Points

  • Complete job tasks: Each task gives 1-3 skill points.
  • Complete job-related achievements: Some achievements give bonus skill points.
  • Reach job milestones: 100 tasks, 500 tasks – you get extra points.

Best Skill Progression Strategy

  • Focus on one job first: Get to 20 points in one job before moving to another.
  • Unlock the best skill first: "Master Delivery" for Pizza Delivery is the best bonus.
  • Use bonus XP events: Farm skill points during double XP events.

Skill Tree Completion Rewards

When you complete all skills in a job, you get a bonus:

  • Pizza Delivery: "Pizza Legend" title + 1,000 cash.
  • Police: "Police Legend" title + 1,000 cash.
  • Firefighter: "Fire Legend" title + 1,000 cash.
  • Cashier: "Cash Legend" title + 1,000 cash.

Pro Tips

  • Start with Pizza Delivery – it's the easiest job to level up.
  • Don't spread your points – focus on one job at a time.
  • Use events – double XP events are the best time to farm skill points.
  • Unlock all 4 jobs – each job gives separate bonuses.
