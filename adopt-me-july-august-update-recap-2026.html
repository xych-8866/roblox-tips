July 2026 Updates

Update 1: Summer Event (Mid-July)

  • New Pets: Coral Crab, Seagull, Sunfish, Palm Tree Golem, Golden Mermaid.
  • New Currency: Summer Shells.
  • New Minigames: Sandcastle Building, Beach Cleanup, Volleyball.
  • Limited Items: Summer Beach House, Palm Tree Stroller.

Update 2: Pet Aging Speed Increase

  • Pet aging speed increased by 20% during the summer event.
  • Better for players who want to make neons and megas.

Update 3: Trade History Feature

  • New "Trade History" feature added – see your past trades.
  • Helps track value changes and trade patterns.

August 2026 Updates

Update 4: Summer Festival (Early August)

  • Limited Items: Summer Crown, Firefly Lantern, Sunflower Stroller.

Update 5: House Upgrades

  • New summer-themed furniture added to the store.
  • More decoration options for players.

Update 6: Daily Reward Bonus

  • Bonus Sunlight Tokens for logging in daily during the event.
  • Incentivizes daily play.
