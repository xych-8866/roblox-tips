How Pet Commands Work

You can command your pet by clicking on it and selecting actions. Some actions are available by default, others require you to teach your pet. Higher-tier pets have more available tricks.

How to Teach Commands

  • Click on your pet – open the interaction menu.
  • Select "Teach Trick" – choose the trick you want to teach.
  • Wait for the teaching animation – it takes 30 seconds.
  • Repeat until unlocked – some tricks take multiple tries.

Potions & Extra Actions

  • Fly Potion: Allows your pet to fly (any rarity).
  • Ride Potion: Allows you to ride your pet (any rarity).
  • Both potions can be applied to any pet – they don't depend on rarity.

Pro Tips

  • Teach tricks early – it increases your pet's bond and happiness.
  • Higher rarity pets have more commands – save your best pets for learning tricks.
  • Fly and Ride potions – they're expensive but add a lot of value to your pet.
  • Use commands during pet aging – they give small XP bonuses.
