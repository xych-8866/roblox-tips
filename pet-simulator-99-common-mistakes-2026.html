Pet Simulator 99 Common Mistakes 2026 – 10 Errors to Avoid

New players often make mistakes that slow down their progress. This guide covers the 10 most common errors in PS99 and how to avoid them.

1. Wasting Diamonds on Basic Eggs

Mistake: Spending diamonds on cracked or basic eggs.

Solution: Use coins for eggs. Save diamonds for enchants and huge pets.

2. Not Getting Diamond V Enchant First

Mistake: Buying other enchants before Diamond V.

Solution: Diamond V is the single most important enchant. Prioritize it above everything else.

3. Farming in Lower Zones Too Long

Mistake: Staying in Grassy Plains or Desert Zone past level 20.

Solution: Move to higher zones as soon as you unlock them – better drops = faster progress.

4. Not Using Potions

Mistake: Forgetting to use Diamond Potions and Speed Potions.

Solution: Always use potions when farming. They double your output.

5. Trading Without Checking Values

Mistake: Accepting trades without checking current values.

Solution: Always check Discord value lists before trading. Don't accept the first offer.

6. Not Collecting Secret Chests Daily

Mistake: Missing daily chests (1,000+ diamonds/day).

Solution: Collect all 5 secret chests daily – it takes 5 minutes.

7. Not Using a Huge Pet

Mistake: Not prioritizing getting a huge pet.

Solution: Huge pets give +100% breakable speed – they're game-changing.

8. Wasting Gems on Low-Tier Enchants

Mistake: Spending gems on Luck V or Glitch V.

Solution: Save gems for Diamonds V, Treasure Hunter V, and Fortune V.

9. Not Joining a Trading Group

Mistake: Trading alone without community input.

Solution: Join a PS99 trading group on Discord – you'll get better deals.

10. Giving Up Too Early

Mistake: Quitting before getting a huge pet.

Solution: PS99 is a grind. Be patient – you'll get there.
