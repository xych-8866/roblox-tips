How Food Works

Food affects your hunger meter. When it drops below 50%, you start losing health. Eating food restores hunger and keeps you healthy. Some foods also give temporary boosts.

Cooking Mechanics

You can cook food in your house! Here's how:

  1. Buy a stove or BBQ from the furniture store.
  2. Place it in your house.
  3. Click the stove and select a food item to cook.
  4. Wait for the cooking timer to finish.
  5. Collect your cooked food – it restores more hunger than store-bought.

Best Foods for Each Situation

  • For hunger: Pizza (best hunger restore).
  • For speed: Coffee (best speed boost).
  • For cheap: Lemonade ($5 – good for early game).
  • For cooking: Cooked steak (restores 70% hunger).

How to Keep Hunger Full

  • Eat pizza regularly – it's the best hunger restore.
  • Drink coffee before driving – speed boost helps.
  • Cook at home – cooked food is cheaper and restores more hunger.
  • Check your hunger meter often – don't let it drop below 50%.

Pro Tips

  • Always carry food – you never know when you'll need it.
  • Cooking at home saves money – cooked food is cheaper than store-bought.
  • Coffee is the best value – speed boost is very useful.
  • Ice cream gives speed – good for short trips.
