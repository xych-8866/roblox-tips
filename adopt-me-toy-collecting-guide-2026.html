Toys are often undervalued by newer players, making them great items to flip. Rare toys can sell for multiple legendary pets, especially limited event toys.

How to Get Rare Toys

  • Event rewards: Complete event quests for exclusive toys.
  • Gifts: Open gifts (any type) – rare toys have a small chance to drop.
  • Trading: Buy undervalued toys from players and resell later.
  • Daily rewards: Some toys are given as daily login rewards.

Toy Flipping Strategy

  1. Buy rare toys during events when prices are low.
  2. Hold for 3-6 months until the event hype is gone.
  3. Sell when demand rises before the next event.
  4. Repeat with different event toys.

Pro Tips

  • Check value lists – toy values fluctuate like pets.
  • Save event toys – they almost always rise in value after events.
  • Don't trade rare toys for low-value pets – hold for better offers.
  • Collect duplicates – having multiple rare toys increases profit potential.
