Adopt Me Hidden Events Guide 2026 – Random Encounters & Secret Rewards

Adopt Me has many hidden events and random encounters that most players never see. This guide reveals every secret event, how to trigger them, and what rewards you can earn.

1. Meteor Shower Event

When: Random night (1-3 AM in-game time).

What happens: Meteors crash near the beach. Click the glowing meteor to collect Stardust (limited item).

Reward: Stardust (tradeable) + "Stargazer" title after collecting 10.

2. Rainbow Fountain Event

When: Random daytime (12 PM – 3 PM in-game time).

What happens: A rainbow fountain appears in the town square. Click it for a random reward – bucks, items, or rare pets.

Reward: Random items – possible legendary pet.

3. Wild Pet Stampede

When: Random daytime (spawns every 4-6 hours).

What happens: A group of wild pets runs through the nursery area. Click one to catch it – you get a free pet.

Reward: Random pet (common to ultra-rare).

4. Ghost Invasion (Halloween Only)

When: Halloween event – random night spawns.

What happens: Ghosts appear around the map. Click them to collect ectoplasm (event currency).

Reward: Ectoplasm – exchange for event items.

5. Present Delivery

When: Random daytime (Christmas event).

What happens: Presents appear around the map. Click them to open – they contain free items.

Reward: Random Christmas items (toys, accessories).

6. Mysterious NPC – The Traveling Merchant

When: Random spawn (once every 3-4 hours).

What happens: A traveling merchant appears near the bridge. Click him to see his inventory – rare items for sale.

Reward: Rare items (limited stock – buy quickly).

7. Secret Fishing Event

When: Random spawn – only during fishing season.

What happens: A rare fish appears in the pond behind the school. Click it to catch it – reward varies.

Reward: Rare fish (sell for bucks or trade).

How to Trigger Hidden Events

  • Be online during specific times – events are tied to in-game time.
  • Explore the map often – events spawn randomly.
  • Check the event board – sometimes events are announced.
  • Stay active – some events require players to be online to trigger.

Pro Tips

  • Private servers – easier to trigger timed events (1-3 AM, 12-3 PM).
  • Meteor Shower is the rarest – Stardust is worth a lot.
  • Traveling Merchant has the best items – save bucks for his inventory.
  • Wild Pet Stampede gives free pets – great for new players.
  • Check for seasonal events – Halloween and Christmas have unique hidden events.
