How Gardening Works

Gardening is a skill in Adopt Me. You buy seeds, plant them, and harvest them for profit. Higher-level plants give better rewards. Each plant requires time and attention to grow.

Best Plants for Profit

  • Rare Rose: Best profit per hour ($1,250/hour).
  • Diamond Flower: Second best ($900/hour).
  • Sunflower: Best for beginners ($600/hour).
  • Pumpkin: Good early game plant ($510/hour).

Garden Strategy

  1. Start with Sunflowers – they're the best beginner plant.
  2. Save for Diamond Flowers – they unlock at gardening level 10.
  3. Plant Rare Roses – they unlock at gardening level 20 (best profit).
  4. Harvest immediately – don't let plants sit after they're ready.
  5. Water regularly – plants grow faster when watered.

Pro Tips

  • Plant in bulk – 10+ plants at once maximizes profit.
  • Use speed potions – they reduce growth time.
  • Water plants regularly – it increases growth speed.
  • Sell harvested crops – don't keep them, sell immediately.
  • Upgrade your garden – more garden space = more plants.
