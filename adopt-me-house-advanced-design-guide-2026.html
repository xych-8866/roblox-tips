Advanced Layout Strategies

1. Open Concept Design

Remove interior walls to create a large living space. Use furniture to define zones:

  • Living area: Sofa, coffee table, TV
  • Dining area: Dining table, chairs, rug
  • Kitchen: Counters, fridge, stove

2. Zoning with Rugs & Lighting

  • Use different rugs to define each zone.
  • Use lighting to create mood – warm lights for living areas, bright lights for kitchens.
  • Add room dividers (bookshelves, screens) for a polished look.

3. Focal Points

  • Each room should have a focal point – a fireplace, large window, TV, or artwork.
  • Arrange furniture facing the focal point.
  • Use wall art to draw attention.

Pro Lighting Tips

  • Layer lighting – combine overhead, task, and accent lighting.
  • Use warm white bulbs for living areas.
  • Use cool white bulbs for kitchens and bathrooms.
  • Add lamps in corners to create a cozy feel.
  • Place chandeliers above dining tables.

Budget Design Tips

  • Check daily free items in the furniture store.
  • Use basic items creatively – a bookshelf can be a room divider.
  • Visit event furniture – limited items are often free or cheap.
  • Focus on one room at a time – finish before moving to the next.

Pro Tips

  • Plan before you buy – sketch your layout first.
  • Use the grid for perfect furniture placement.
  • Save your design – take screenshots as you go.
  • Showcase your house – invite friends over.
