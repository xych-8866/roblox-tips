Best Quest Strategy

Daily Quests

  1. Complete the Kill 30 NPCs quest – it's the fastest (5-10 min).
  2. Do Defeat 1 Boss – Darkbeard or Gorilla King are quick.
  3. If you have time, do Collect 5 Chests – run a chest route.
  4. Avoid Complete 1 Sea Event if you're short on time – it's the longest.

Weekly Quests

  1. Start with Kill 500 NPCs – it's the most consistent.
  2. Do Complete 10 Sea Events – combine with daily sea event quest.
  3. If you have time, do Defeat 5 Bosses – spread it across the week.

Pro Tips

  • Do daily quests every day – they add up to 1M+ Beli per month.
  • Combine quests – do daily sea event + weekly sea event together.
  • Use Buddha fruit – fastest way to kill NPCs for quests.
  • Server hop for bosses – spawn them faster.
  • Save weekly quests for Sunday – if you haven't completed them, rush them before reset.
