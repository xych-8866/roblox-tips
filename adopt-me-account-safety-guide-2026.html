Adopt Me Account Safety Guide 2026 – How to Protect Your Account

Account safety is crucial in Adopt Me. This guide covers the most common scams, how to secure your account, and what to do if you get scammed.

Common Scams to Avoid

1. The Trust Trade Scam

How it works: A player asks you to trade them your pet, promising to return it with extra items. They never return it.

How to avoid: Never accept a trust trade. All trades should be completed in the official trade window.

2. The Fake Overpay Scam

How it works: A player offers an insane overpay (e.g., a Shadow Dragon for a Dog). They remove the valuable pet and accept quickly, hoping you don't notice.

How to avoid: Always check the trade window carefully before accepting. Take your time.

3. The "Trusted Middleman" Scam

How it works: A scammer poses as a "trusted middleman" who will hold pets during a trade. They steal the pets and disappear.

How to avoid: Never use a middleman. Adopt Me's trade system handles trades securely without needing a third party.

4. The Free Robux Scam

How it works: Scammers offer free Robux in exchange for pets. They send a link that leads to a fake Roblox login page to steal your account.

How to avoid: Never click on suspicious links. Roblox never asks for your password outside the official login page.

5. The "Pet Duplication" Scam

How it works: Scammers claim they can duplicate pets. They ask you to trade them the pet, promising to give you two back. They never return it.

How to avoid: There is no pet duplication glitch. Never trade a pet for duplication promises.

How to Secure Your Account

  • Enable 2-Step Verification: Turn on 2FA (two-factor authentication) on your Roblox account.
  • Use a strong password: At least 12 characters, mix of letters, numbers, and symbols.
  • Never share your password: Roblox will never ask for your password outside the login page.
  • Check your login history: If you see unfamiliar locations, change your password immediately.
  • Log out after playing: Especially on shared devices.

What to Do If You Get Scammed

  1. Report the scammer to Adopt Me support immediately.
  2. Report the user on the Roblox platform.
  3. Change your password if you shared any information.
  4. Learn from the experience – don't let it ruin your day.

Pro Tips

  • Trust your gut: If it feels wrong, it probably is.
  • Don't trade with new accounts: They're often scammers.
  • Check the trade window twice: Before hitting accept.
  • Block and report: Suspicious players to Adopt Me support.
  • Educate your friends: Share these tips to keep them safe too.
