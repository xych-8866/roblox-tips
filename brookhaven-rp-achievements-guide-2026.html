Brookhaven RP Achievements Guide 2026 – All Rewards & How to Unlock

Brookhaven RP has 20 hidden achievements that give cash and exclusive titles. This guide covers every achievement and how to unlock them.

How Achievements Work

Achievements are unlocked by completing specific tasks. Most are hidden – you won't see them until you unlock them. Each gives a reward (cash, title, or item).

All Achievements

1. "The Explorer"

How to unlock: Visit every location on the map (20+ locations) in one session without teleporting.

Reward: "Explorer" title + 500 cash.

2. "The Speedster"

How to unlock: Complete a pizza delivery in under 20 seconds.

Reward: "Speedster" title + 200 cash.

3. "The Generous"

How to unlock: Gift 10 items to other players (worth at least 100 cash each).

Reward: "Generous" title + 500 cash.

4. "The Investor"

How to unlock: Have 50,000 cash in the bank at one time.

Reward: "Investor" title + 1,000 cash.

5. "The Fashionista"

How to unlock: Own 50 unique clothing/accessory items.

Reward: "Fashionista" title + 300 cash.

6. "The Collector"

How to unlock: Own 10 vehicles.

Reward: "Collector" title + 500 cash.

7. "The Host"

How to unlock: Host a party with at least 5 guests.

Reward: "Host" title + 200 cash.

8. "The Party Animal"

How to unlock: Attend 10 parties.

Reward: "Party Animal" title + 300 cash.

9. "The Cop"

How to unlock: Arrest 50 criminals as a police officer.

Reward: "Cop" title + 500 cash.

10. "The Firefighter"

How to unlock: Put out 50 fires as a firefighter.

Reward: "Firefighter" title + 500 cash.

11. "The Millionaire"

How to unlock: Earn 1,000,000 total cash (lifetime).

Reward: "Millionaire" title + 1,000 cash.

12. "The Builder"

How to unlock: Place 100 furniture items in your house.

Reward: "Builder" title + 300 cash.

13. "The Driver"

How to unlock: Drive 1,000 miles total.

Reward: "Driver" title + 500 cash.

14. "The Hero"

How to unlock: Save 10 players from drowning as a lifeguard.

Reward: "Hero" title + 500 cash.

15. "The Teacher"

How to unlock: Complete 50 tasks at the school (as a teacher).

Reward: "Teacher" title + 300 cash.

16. "The Delivery Guy"

How to unlock: Complete 500 pizza deliveries.

Reward: "Delivery Guy" title + 1,000 cash.

17. "The Gambler"

How to unlock: Win 10 games in the arcade.

Reward: "Gambler" title + 200 cash.

18. "The Explorer"

How to unlock: Find all 7 secret rooms.

Reward: "Explorer" title + 500 cash.

19. "The Pet Lover"

How to unlock: Care for 100 pets in the nursery.

Reward: "Pet Lover" title + 300 cash.

20. "The Legend"

How to unlock: Unlock all 19 other achievements.

Reward: "Legend" title + 2,000 cash.

Best Achievements to Unlock First

  • The Investor: 1,000 cash – save up 50k.
  • The Delivery Guy: 1,000 cash – just do pizza deliveries.
  • The Cop/Firefighter: 500 cash each – do job tasks.
  • The Explorer: 500 cash – visit all locations.

Pro Tips

  • Start with easy ones – The Explorer, The Generous, and The Party Animal are the easiest.
  • Track your progress – achievements are hidden, but you can check your stats in the menu.
  • Grind jobs – many achievements require job tasks (Cop, Firefighter, Delivery Guy).
  • Unlock "The Legend" – it's the ultimate achievement.
