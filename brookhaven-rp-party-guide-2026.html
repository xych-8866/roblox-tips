Brookhaven RP Party Guide 2026 – How to Throw the Best Party

Throwing parties is one of the best ways to enjoy Brookhaven RP with friends. This guide covers everything – how to invite friends, decorations, party games, and exclusive party items.

1. How to Throw a Party

  1. Go to your house.
  2. Click the "Party" button in the menu.
  3. Set the time, date, and privacy (public or invite-only).
  4. Invite friends from your friend list or in-game.
  5. Decorate your house – add balloons, confetti, and music.
  6. Wait for guests to arrive and enjoy!

3. Party Games to Play

  • Hide and Seek: Classic game – one player hides, others seek.
  • Fashion Show: Players dress up and vote on the best outfit.
  • Dance Off: Players dance in front of the disco ball – winner gets applause.
  • Trivia: Ask Brookhaven-related questions – winner gets bragging rights.
  • Racing: Use vehicles and race across the map.

4. Exclusive Party Items

Some items can only be obtained during parties:

  • Party Hat: Received when you attend a party (free).
  • Confetti Cannon: Rare drop from party chests.
  • Party Crown: Given to the host of a successful party (requires 5+ guests).
  • Birthday Cake: Special item for birthday parties.

5. Best Party Tips

  • Decorate early – set up decorations before inviting guests.
  • Plan activities – have a schedule of games to keep guests engaged.
  • Take photos – capture the fun moments.
  • Give rewards – gift small items to guests for participating.
  • Keep it fun – don't stress about perfection, just enjoy it.

Pro Tips

  • Invite at least 5 friends – you'll get the Party Crown reward.
  • Set a theme (beach party, costume party, formal party) – it makes it more fun.
  • Use music – it sets the mood.
  • Check the party calendar in the menu – sometimes events give special party items.
