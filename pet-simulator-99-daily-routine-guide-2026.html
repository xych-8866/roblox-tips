Pet Simulator 99 Daily Routine Guide 2026 – Maximize Your Daily Profit

To progress fast in PS99, you need a consistent daily routine. This guide provides a step-by-step plan to maximize diamonds, coins, and progress every day.

Your Daily Routine (15-30 Minutes)

Follow this routine every day for maximum progress:

Step 1: Login Reward (1 minute)

Login and claim your daily login reward. Day 7 gives 500 diamonds, Day 14 gives 1,000, Day 30 gives 5,000.

Step 2: Secret Chests (5 minutes)

Collect all 5 secret chests:

  • Tech World – 250 diamonds
  • Fantasy World – 250 diamonds
  • Void World – 500 diamonds
  • Jungle Zone – 100 diamonds
  • Ice Zone – 200 diamonds

Total: ~1,300 diamonds in 5 minutes.

Step 3: Daily Quests (10 minutes)

Complete all 3 daily quests:

  • Break 500 breakables – 100 diamonds
  • Hatch 10 eggs – 150 diamonds
  • Trade 3 times – 200 diamonds

Total: 450 diamonds + 500 bonus (if all completed).

Step 4: Coin Jar Farming (10 minutes)

Run the Tech World → Void World route, breaking coin jars. Each jar gives 5-20 diamonds. Total: ~200-300 diamonds.

Step 5: Active Farming (Optional)

If you have extra time, farm Void World with Diamond Potion and Speed Potion for 30 minutes. Total: ~100,000+ diamonds.

Weekly Bonuses

  • Weekend Events: 2x diamonds from all activities.
  • Weekly Quests: 1,000+ diamonds for completing all weekly quests.
  • Hour Chests: Claim hourly for free diamonds (set a timer).

Pro Tips

  • Do the routine daily – consistency is key.
  • Combine steps – do Coin Jar Farming while completing daily quests.
  • Use alt accounts – more accounts = more diamonds from chests.
  • Save diamonds for enchants – don't waste them on eggs.
  • Check event board – event quests give bonus rewards.
