Blox Fruits Rebirth Guide 2026 – Reset & Start Over Smart

Rebirth (prestige) resets your level but gives you permanent bonuses. This guide explains what you keep, what you lose, and the best time to reset your progress.

What Is Rebirth?

Rebirth (also called Prestige) resets your level to 1 but gives you permanent bonuses. It's a way to get stronger over time. You can rebirth multiple times.

What You Keep

  • ✅ All your fruits (Beli, fragments, etc.)
  • ✅ All your accessories and swords
  • ✅ All your game passes
  • ✅ All your fighting styles
  • ✅ All your race progress
  • ✅ All your Beli and fragments

What You Lose

  • ❌ All your levels (resets to 1)
  • ❌ All stat points (resets – you need to reassign)
  • ❌ All mission progress

When to Rebirth

  • Best time: When you reach max level (2550).
  • Second best time: When you have enough Beli/fragments to buy back your essential items.
  • Worst time: Early game (below level 1000) – not worth it.

Why Rebirth?

  • Rebirth 1: +5% damage (permanent).
  • Rebirth 2: +5% damage (total 10%).
  • Rebirth 3: +5% damage (total 15%).
  • Rebirth 4: +5% damage (total 20%).
  • Rebirth 5: +5% damage (total 25%).

How to Rebirth

  1. Reach max level (2550).
  2. Talk to the Rebirth NPC in Third Sea.
  3. Click "Rebirth" – confirm your choice.
  4. You reset to level 1 but keep all items.

Post-Rebirth Strategy

  1. Reassign your stat points – focus on the build you want.
  2. Use Buddha fruit to grind fast – it's still the best grinding fruit.
  3. Equip Ghoul Mask and Dark Coat – they help you level quickly.
  4. Follow your grinding route from level 1 again – you'll level much faster with stat points already assigned.

Pro Tips

  • Don't rebirth early – it's a waste of time.
  • Save Beli before rebirthing – you'll need it to buy items.
  • Rebirth only at max level – otherwise you're just slowing yourself down.
  • Use Buddha fruit – it makes leveling after rebirth much faster.
  • Rebirth up to 5 times – each gives +5% damage, max 25%.
