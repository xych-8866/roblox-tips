Adopt Me Fly & Ride Potions Guide 2026 – Best Pets to Potion

Fly and Ride potions are some of the most valuable items in Adopt Me. This guide covers everything you need to know – which pets to use them on, trading values, and the best potion strategies.

What Are Fly & Ride Potions?

  • Ride Potion: Allows you to ride your pet. Cost: 150 Robux (in-game) or ~2-3 legendary pets (trading).
  • Fly Potion: Allows your pet to fly. Cost: 295 Robux (in-game) or ~5-8 legendary pets (trading).
  • Both potions can be applied to any pet – they don't depend on rarity.

Potion Strategy

Strategy 1: Potion Flipping

Buy potions cheap and resell for more:

  • Buy Ride Potions for 1-2 legendary pets, sell for 3-4 during peak demand (weekends, events).
  • Buy Fly Potions for 5-6 legendary pets, sell for 7-8 when demand is high.
  • Profit margin: 50-100% per flip.

Strategy 2: Potion + Pet Combo

Apply potions to pets and sell for a premium:

  • Buy a pet + Ride Potion (combine) → sell for 3-4x the pet's base value.
  • Buy a pet + Fly Potion (combine) → sell for 2-3x the pet's base value.
  • Best pets to combo: Mid-tier legendaries (Turtle, Kangaroo, Unicorn).

Strategy 3: Hold During Events

Potion values drop during events (players sell cheap for event currency). Buy during events, hold for 2-3 weeks, sell at regular price.

When to Use Potions

  • Use potions on your best pets – it increases their value.
  • Use on pets you plan to keep – if you're not trading, potions add fun.
  • Use Ride Potion on mid-tier pets – increases demand and value.
  • Use Fly Potion on high-tier pets – it multiplies their value.

Pro Tips

  • Buy potions during events – they're cheaper.
  • Don't waste potions on commons – not worth it.
  • Save potions for trading – they're valuable currency.
  • Check value lists – potion values change after updates.
