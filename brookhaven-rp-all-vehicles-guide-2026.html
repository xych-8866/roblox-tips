Hidden Vehicles

  • Police Interceptor: Inside police station garage – complete "Speed Trap" quest (catch 5 speeders).
  • Vintage Convertible: Behind museum – spawns only at night (10 PM – 5 AM).
  • Beach Buggy: Under the pier – click to claim.
  • Abandoned Muscle Car: Behind gas station – click repeatedly until engine starts.
  • Ice Cream Truck: Random spawn near playground – must claim before it despawns (2 min).
  • Mini UFO: Gas station roof – click antenna 5 times.

Best Vehicle for Each Purpose

  • Speed: Sports Car (fastest in dealership).
  • Free Speed: Police Interceptor (best free vehicle).
  • Budget: Compact Car ($8,000).
  • Collector: Mini UFO (rarest vehicle).
  • Everyday: Sedan ($15,000).

Pro Tips

  • Buy a car early – it saves time traveling.
  • Unlock hidden vehicles – they're free and some are very fast.
  • Save for Sports Car – it's the fastest dealership car.
  • Check dealership daily – limited vehicles appear sometimes.
