The PS99 Market – How It Works

The PS99 trading market is driven by supply and demand. Understanding these dynamics is the key to making consistent profits.

  • Supply: How many of a pet exist. Limited event pets have low supply.
  • Demand: How many players want it. Huge pets always have high demand.
  • Liquidity: How quickly a pet can be sold. Huge pets have high liquidity.

Market Analysis Tools

  • Discord Value Lists: The most up-to-date prices. Join multiple servers for comparison.
  • Trading Plaza: Watch what people are buying and selling.
  • Event Calendar: Upcoming events affect prices. Buy before events, sell after.
  • Update Logs: New pets, reworks, and balance changes affect values.

Strategy 1: Arbitrage (Cross-Server Trading)

Different servers have different prices. Here's how to profit from this:

  1. Find a pet undervalued in one server (check multiple trading hubs).
  2. Buy it quickly before others notice.
  3. Switch to another server and sell it at a higher price.
  4. Repeat with different pets.

Best items for arbitrage: Exclusives, rare enchants (Diamonds V), and limited pets.

Strategy 2: Event Flipping (Long-Term Profit)

This strategy requires patience but pays off well:

  1. During event: Buy event pets cheap from players who need quick cash.
  2. 1-2 months after event: Prices rise as supply decreases.
  3. Before next event: Sell at peak price.

Example: Golden Mermaid bought for 50B during Summer 2026, sold for 150B+ after 3 months.

Strategy 4: The "Trade-Up" Method (Consistent)

Start with low-value pets and gradually trade up:

  1. 4 common pets → 1 uncommon
  2. 4 uncommon → 1 rare
  3. 4 rare → 1 ultra-rare
  4. 4 ultra-rare → 1 legendary
  5. 4 legendaries → 1 huge pet

This takes time but is the most consistent way to get huge pets without spending diamonds.

Risk Management

  • Don't invest everything in one pet – diversify your portfolio.
  • Never trade huge pets for multiple exclusives – huge pets are always more valuable.
  • Watch for updates – pets that are getting buffs will rise in value.
  • Sell before major events – prices drop right before new events.

Pro Tips

  • Check multiple Discord servers – different servers have different value lists.
  • Trade during events – players sell cheap to get event currency.
  • Use alt accounts – more accounts = more event pets = more profit.
  • Be patient – the best trades take weeks, not minutes.
