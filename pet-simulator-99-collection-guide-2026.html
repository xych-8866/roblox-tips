How to Track Your Collection

  • Open the Collection menu (click the book icon).
  • See which pets you have and which you're missing.
  • Click on missing pets – it shows where to get them.
  • Check the progress bar – shows completion percentage.

Best Strategy for Collections

  • Start with zone collections – easiest to complete.
  • Then event collections – they give the best rewards.
  • Save legendaries for last – they're the hardest to get.
  • Trade duplicates – use the Trading Plaza to get missing pets.

Collection Rewards Summary

  • Each collection gives diamonds + a unique title.
  • Completing a rarity collection gives bonus diamonds.
  • Completing the Huge collection gives the best reward (10,000 diamonds).
  • Some collections give exclusive items (hats, accessories).

Pro Tips

  • Don't sell duplicate event pets – they count toward collections.
  • Trade for missing pets – it's faster than hatching.
  • Complete zone collections first – they're the easiest.
  • Check collections before trading – don't trade a pet you need.
