Adopt Me Hidden Pets Guide 2026 – All Secret Pets & How to Get Them

Beyond the standard eggs, Adopt Me has hidden pets that most players never find. This guide reveals every secret pet in the game, how to unlock them, and their current trading values.

1. Ghost Dog (Secret Event Pet)

How to get: Take the elevator to the hospital basement. Click the glowing green button – a Ghost Dog spawns. Must be done at night.

Rarity: Rare

Trading Value: ~3-5 Legendary Pets

2. Rock (Secret Pet)

How to get: Click the rock near the bridge 50 times. It turns into a Rock pet.

Rarity: Common

Trading Value: Low (collector item)

3. Shadow Dog (Night Exclusive)

How to get: Visit the cemetery at 3 AM in-game time. A Shadow Dog appears behind the large gravestone. Click it to adopt.

Rarity: Ultra-Rare

Trading Value: ~5-8 Legendary Pets

4. Ice Penguin (Secret Winter Pet)

How to get: During winter events, go to the frozen pond behind the school. Click the ice block 10 times – a penguin appears. Only available during winter update.

Rarity: Rare

Trading Value: ~2-3 Legendary Pets (seasonal)

5. Golden Cat (Gift Exclusive)

How to get: Open 100 gift boxes (any type). The 100th gift has a chance to drop a Golden Cat.

Rarity: Ultra-Rare

Trading Value: ~4-6 Legendary Pets

6. Alien (Secret UFO Pet)

How to get: At 2 AM in-game time, look at the sky – a UFO appears. Click it quickly before it disappears. The Alien drops and can be adopted.

Rarity: Legendary

Trading Value: ~8-10 Legendary Pets

7. Skeleton Dog (Halloween Exclusive)

How to get: During Halloween, visit the cemetery and click all 5 glowing tombstones. The Skeleton Dog spawns.

Rarity: Ultra-Rare

Trading Value: ~3-5 Legendary Pets (seasonal)
