Evolution increases your pet's base stats. Each evolution level gives a permanent stat boost. You need specific materials to evolve – some are common, others are rare.

Best Pets to Evolve

  • Huge Pets: Highest stat boost – prioritize these.
  • Legendaries: Good boost, worth evolving if you use them.
  • Event Pets: Limited availability – evolve them before they disappear.
  • Rainbow Pets: Already strong – evolving makes them even stronger.

Evolution Strategy

  1. Farm Shards: Grind breakables in Void World for the most shards per hour.
  2. Save Crystals: They're rare – don't waste on low-tier pets.
  3. Prioritize Huge Pets: They get the biggest boost from evolution.
  4. Evolve in order: Evolution 1 → Evolution 2 → Evolution 3 (don't skip).

Pro Tips

  • Farm shards in Void World – it gives the most shards per breakable.
  • Save crystals for huge pets – they're too valuable for low-tier pets.
  • Evolve during events – sometimes evolution costs are reduced.
  • Don't evolve commons – it's a waste of materials.
  • Evolution 5 gives +75% damage – it's a huge power spike.
