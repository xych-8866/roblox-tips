How Fishing Works

Fishing is an activity you can do in Brookhaven RP. You need a fishing rod (buy from the store for $500). Click the water to cast your line, wait for a bite, then click again to reel it in. Different fish give different rewards.

Best Fishing Spots

  • Pond (behind school): Best for beginners – common and uncommon fish.
  • River (near campground): Good for medium fish and rare catches.
  • Lake (near the pier): Best for large fish and golden fish.
  • Ocean (beach area): Best for golden fish and legendary fish (requires boat).
  • Secret Pond (hidden location): Best for rainbow fish – requires exploration.

Fishing Tips

  • Fish during summer: Rare fish spawn more often.
  • Fish at night: Golden fish only spawn at night.
  • Upgrade your rod: Better rods = better catch rates (available at the store).
  • Use bait: Bait increases your chances of catching rare fish.
  • Fish in different spots: Each spot has different fish.

Pro Tips

  • Start at the pond: It's the easiest spot for beginners.
  • Save up for a better rod: It pays for itself quickly.
  • Fish during events: Some events have bonus fish rewards.
  • Collect all fish types: Complete the collection for the "Legendary Fisher" title.
