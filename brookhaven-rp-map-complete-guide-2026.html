Map Regions

The map is divided into several regions. Each region has unique features, NPCs, and hidden spots.

1. Town Center

Key locations:

  • Bank – store cash
  • Police Station – police officer job
  • Hospital – heal, medical kit
  • School – pet aging (tasks)
  • Museum – hidden vault

Best for: Banking, jobs, and hidden rooms.

2. Residential Area

Key locations:

  • Houses (starter to mansion)
  • Nursery – adopt pets
  • Furniture Store – buy decoration items
  • Church – hidden secrets

Best for: Buying houses and decorating.

3. Commercial District

Key locations:

  • Pizza Place – pizza delivery job
  • Grocery Store – buy food
  • Gas Station – refuel cars
  • Bowling Alley – minigames
  • Arcade – token games

Best for: Jobs, food, and minigames.

4. Beach Area

Key locations:

  • Beach – swimming, hidden cave
  • Lifeguard Tower – lifeguard job
  • Pier – hidden buggy
  • Ice Cream Shop – buy food

Best for: Summer activities and hidden items.

5. Park Area

Key locations:

  • Playground – swings, slides
  • Campground – camping chair (AFK)
  • Basketball Court – minigame
  • Skate Park – minigame

Best for: Recreation and minigames.

6. Industrial Area

Key locations:

  • Fire Station – firefighter job
  • Police Station (back) – hidden basement
  • Gas Station (back) – hidden car

Best for: Jobs and hidden items.

Fast Travel Tips

  • Buy a fast car – reduces travel time significantly.
  • Use teleport commands – "/home" to go home, "/spawn" to respawn.
  • Collect hidden stashes while traveling – they're everywhere.
  • Use shortcuts – alleys and paths connect regions faster.

Pro Tips

  • Learn the map – knowing locations saves time.
  • Use waypoints – mark important locations.
  • Explore often – new secret spots are added regularly.
  • Check the map icon – in the menu, it shows your current location.
