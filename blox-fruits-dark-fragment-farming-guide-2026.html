Blox Fruits Dark Fragment Farming Guide 2026 – Fastest Methods & Best Spots

Dark fragments are essential for upgrading dark weapons and crafting rare items. This guide covers every method to farm dark fragments, including boss drops, chests, and NPC farming.

What Are Dark Fragments Used For?

  • Dark Dagger upgrades: Upgrade your dark dagger to max level.
  • Dark Coating: Craft dark coating for armor boosts.
  • Trade value: Dark fragments are valuable in the trading market.

Method 1: Darkbeard Boss (Best Method)

Location: Frozen Village (First Sea) – spawns at night.

Spawn Time: Night (10 PM – 5 AM in-game).

Dark Fragments per kill: 10-20

Rewards: Dark Coat (accessory) + 10k Beli + dark fragments.

Strategy: Use Magma or Buddha fruit. Farm Darkbeard every night cycle. Server hop after each kill to respawn faster.

Method 2: Dark NPCs (Second Sea)

Location: Kingdom of Rose (Second Sea) – dark-themed NPCs spawn at night.

Dark Fragments per kill: 1-3

Strategy: Use Buddha fruit for AOE farming. Stay in areas with high NPC density. This method is slower but consistent.

Method 3: Dark Chests (Third Sea)

Location: Third Sea – dark chests spawn in specific locations.

Dark Fragments per chest: 5-15

Best route: Start at Great Tree → Sea of Treats → Mansion → Cake Land. Check each dark chest spawn point.

Method 4: Rogue Sea Beast (Night Spawn)

Location: Third Sea – only at night.

Dark Fragments per kill: 20-30

Strategy: Requires a large crew. Use Magma fruit for sea event damage. Focus on the glowing spot on its back.

Method 5: Trading

How it works: Buy dark fragments from other players for Beli or fruits.

Average price: 10k Beli per fragment (varies by server).

Best for: Players with excess Beli or fruits.

Best Overall Strategy

  1. Farm Darkbeard every night cycle – it's the most consistent.
  2. Combine with Dark NPCs during daytime – fill the gap.
  3. Check Dark Chests in Third Sea – they add up over time.
  4. If you have a crew, farm Rogue Sea Beast at night – it gives the most fragments per hour.

Pro Tips

  • Server hop – after killing Darkbeard, switch servers to respawn instantly.
  • Use Buddha fruit – it's the best for farming dark NPCs.
  • Save dark fragments – don't spend them until you have enough for a full upgrade.
  • Check the trading hub – sometimes players sell dark fragments cheap.
