How Secret Achievements Work

Secret achievements are hidden tasks that aren't listed in the main menu. You unlock them by doing specific actions. Each gives a reward – usually diamonds, titles, or exclusive items.

1. "The Collector"

How to unlock: Own 100 unique pets (no duplicates).

Reward: 5,000 diamonds + "Collector" title.

2. "Master Hatch"

How to unlock: Hatch 500 eggs total.

Reward: Lucky Egg (2x hatch luck for 1 hour) + "Hatch Master" title.

3. "Diamond Miner"

How to unlock: Earn 1,000,000 diamonds total (lifetime).

Reward: Diamond Crown accessory (+5% diamond farm) + 10,000 diamonds.

4. "Event Champion"

How to unlock: Complete 10 event quests in one event.

Reward: Exclusive event pet + "Champion" title.

5. "Ultimate Trader"

How to unlock: Complete 100 trades in Trading Plaza.

Reward: 5,000 diamonds + "Trader" title.

6. "Fusion Master"

How to unlock: Perform 50 fusions.

Reward: Fusion Boost (+50% fusion speed) + "Fusion Master" title.

7. "Enchant Collector"

How to unlock: Own 10 different enchants (any tier).

Reward: 2,000 diamonds + "Enchanter" title.

8. "Secret Finder"

How to unlock: Find all 5 secret chests in one day.

Reward: 1,000 diamonds + "Explorer" title.

9. "The Millionaire"

How to unlock: Have 1,000,000 diamonds in your inventory at once.

Reward: Golden Crown accessory + "Millionaire" title.

10. "Completionist"

How to unlock: Unlock all 9 other secret achievements.

Reward: 50,000 diamonds + "Completionist" title + exclusive huge pet.

Best Secret Achievements to Get First

  • Secret Finder: Easiest – just collect 5 chests in one day.

Pro Tips

  • Track your progress – use the achievement tracker in the menu.
  • Save diamonds – some achievements require saving up.
  • Trade often – Ultimate Trader is easy if you trade frequently.
  • Fuse regularly – Fusion Master requires 50 fusions.
