Adopt Me Baby Guide 2026 – Everything About Babies & Parenting

Having a baby in Adopt Me isn't just for roleplay – it has real gameplay benefits. This guide covers everything about babies, parenting tasks, and the rewards you can earn.

How to Have a Baby

  1. Go to the nursery (or your house).
  2. Click the "Have Baby" button in the menu.
  3. Choose a name for your baby.
  4. Your baby appears – you're now a parent!

Parenting Rewards

Caring for your baby has several benefits:

  • XP: Each task gives 10-15 XP towards your parenting level.
  • Bucks: 2-3 bucks per task – passive income while raising babies.
  • Titles: Achievements give parenting titles.
  • Items: Some parenting achievements unlock exclusive items.

Best Baby Strategy

  • Have a baby as soon as possible – it gives passive XP and bucks.
  • Do tasks regularly – babies need attention just like pets.
  • Use baby for 2x speed – having a baby also gives 2x task speed for pets!
  • Complete parenting achievements – they give exclusive rewards.

Pro Tips

  • Always have a baby equipped – 2x task speed is huge.
  • Use baby for pet aging – the 2x speed applies to pets too.
  • Complete tasks regularly – they add up over time.
  • Don't let baby needs drop – unhappy babies give less XP.
