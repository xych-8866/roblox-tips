Pet Simulator 99 Zero to Huge in 7 Days – Complete F2P Guide 2026

Getting a huge pet in PS99 without spending money seems impossible, but it's not. This 7-day guide takes you from zero to owning your first huge pet – no Robux, just strategy.

Your 7-Day Plan

Each day has a specific goal. Follow the plan exactly – don't skip steps.

Day 1: Get Started

  • Hatch your starter egg – you get 3 common pets.
  • Complete the tutorial – it gives coins and unlocks basic areas.
  • Grind Grassy Plains until you unlock Desert Zone.
  • Buy the Coin V enchant – this is your first priority.
  • Goal: Reach level 15, unlock 3 pet slots, save 10,000 coins.

Day 2: Build Your Farm

  • Unlock Ice Zone – better coin and diamond drops.
  • Equip Coin V (if you got it) – start farming coins efficiently.
  • Buy 2-3 new pets from the egg machine – improve your damage.
  • Complete daily quests for bonus rewards.
  • Goal: Reach level 25, unlock 4 pet slots, save 5,000 diamonds.

Day 3: First Diamond Farm

  • Unlock Tech World – the first zone with good diamond drops.
  • Buy Diamonds V enchant – this is your most important purchase.
  • Start farming diamonds actively – at least 30 minutes/day.
  • Collect secret chests – Tech World chest gives 250 diamonds (every 4 hours).
  • Goal: Save 20,000 diamonds.

Day 4: Enchant Upgrades

  • Buy Treasure Hunter V enchant – more breakables = more drops.
  • Unlock Void World – the best diamond farming zone.
  • Farm Void World with Diamond Potion (bought with diamonds).
  • Collect all 5 secret chests daily – 1,000+ diamonds/day.
  • Goal: Save 80,000 diamonds.

Day 5: Power Farm

  • Fully equip Diamonds V + Treasure Hunter V + Fortune V enchants.
  • Use Diamond Potion + Speed Potion – stack them for max output.
  • Farm Void World for 2 hours – aim for 100,000+ diamonds.
  • Goal: Save 200,000 diamonds.

Day 6: First Huge Pet

  • You should have 200,000-300,000 diamonds saved.
  • Go to Trading Plaza – buy the cheapest huge pet you can find.
  • Target: Huge Snowman (~80B) or Huge Jelly Pig (~125B).
  • Equip your huge pet – +100% breakable speed.
  • Goal: Own at least 1 huge pet.

Day 7: Scale Up

  • Use your huge pet to farm even faster – diamonds/hour doubles.
  • Save for better huge pets – target Huge Cupcake (~360B) or better.
  • Start trading – you now have a valuable asset.
  • Goal: Build a collection of 2-3 huge pets.

How to Speed Up Each Day

  • Farm during weekend events – double diamond and coin events speed everything up.
  • Use an auto-clicker – if allowed, it keeps you farming while AFK.
  • Join a trading group – better deals on huge pets.
  • Don't waste diamonds – every diamond should go toward enchants or huge pets.

Pro Tips

  • Prioritize Diamonds V – it's the single most important enchant.
  • Save gems for enchants – don't spend them on eggs.
  • Collect secret chests daily – they're free diamonds.
  • Huge pets are game-changers – once you get one, everything becomes faster.
