Blox Fruits Complete Islands Guide 2026 – All Islands, Secrets & Best Grinding Spots

Blox Fruits has three seas with dozens of islands. This complete guide covers every island in the game, including best grinding spots, hidden chests, NPC locations, and secrets you might have missed.

First Sea Islands (Level 1-700)

1. Starter Island

Level Range: 1-50

NPCs: Bandits (level 5-15)

Best Grinding Spot: Bandit camp near spawn – respawns quickly.

Hidden Chest: Behind the waterfall near the bridge – contains 500 Beli.

2. Jungle

Level Range: 50-150

NPCs: Jungle Bandits (level 20-40)

Best Grinding Spot: Jungle temple – bandits spawn in groups.

Hidden Chest: Inside temple behind stone statue – 1,000 Beli.

Boss: Gorilla King (spawns every 15 min) – drops 5k Beli + common fruit.

3. Pirate Village

Level Range: 100-250

NPCs: Pirates (level 40-70)

Best Grinding Spot: Behind the bar – pirates spawn in clusters.

Hidden Chest: In the well behind the bar – 500 Beli.

4. Desert

Level Range: 200-400

NPCs: Desert Bandits (level 70-120)

Best Grinding Spot: Near the oasis – desert bandits spawn in groups of 3-4.

Hidden Chest: Inside the pyramid – requires puzzle.

5. Frozen Village

Level Range: 350-650

NPCs: Yetis (level 120-200)

Best Grinding Spot: Ice cave – yetis spawn in groups of 4-5.

Hidden Chest: Behind the ice waterfall – 2,000 Beli.

Boss: Darkbeard (night spawn) – Dark Coat + 30 fragments.

Tip: This is the best grinding spot in First Sea – stay here until level 600.

6. Sky Islands

Level Range: 600-700

NPCs: Sky Bandits (level 200-250)

Best Grinding Spot: Cloud platform near teleporter.

Hidden Chest: On the highest cloud – 500 Beli.

Second Sea Islands (Level 700-1500)

7. Kingdom of Rose

Level Range: 700-900

NPCs: Pirates (level 250-300)

Best Grinding Spot: The bridge area – pirates spawn constantly.

Hidden Chest: Behind the castle underwater – 3,000 Beli.

Boss: Diamond – drops diamond accessories.

8. Ice Castle

Level Range: 800-1000

NPCs: Ice Guards (level 300-350)

Best Grinding Spot: Inside the castle – ice guards spawn in groups.

Hidden Chest: In dungeon behind breakable wall – 2,000 Beli.

Boss: Awakened Ice Admiral – 50 fragments + Winter Cape.

9. Green Zone

Level Range: 1000-1200

NPCs: Elite Pirates (level 350-400)

Best Grinding Spot: Forest area – elite pirates in groups.

Hidden Chest: Under giant tree – 5,000 Beli.

Boss: Venom (rare spawn) – drops Venom fruit.

10. Floating Turtle

Level Range: 1200-1500

NPCs: Turtle Soldiers (level 400-450)

Best Grinding Spot: The shell area – turtle soldiers in large groups.

Hidden Chest: Underneath the turtle underwater – 10,000 Beli.

Third Sea Islands (Level 1500-2550)

11. Great Tree

Level Range: 1500-1700

NPCs: Acolytes (level 450-500)

Best Grinding Spot: Around the base of the tree – acolytes spawn in groups.

Hidden Chest: Inside the tree hollow – 5,000 Beli.

12. Sea of Treats

Level Range: 1700-1900

NPCs: Candy Soldiers (level 500-550)

Best Grinding Spot: Candy village – candy soldiers in clusters.

Hidden Chest: Inside gingerbread house – 10,000 Beli.

Boss: Dough King (requires Dough raid) – Dough fruit + 250 fragments.

13. Mansion

Level Range: 1900-2100

NPCs: Ghost NPCs (level 550-600)

Best Grinding Spot: Inside the mansion – ghost NPCs in large groups.

Hidden Chest: Secret room behind bookshelf – 15,000 Beli.

Boss: Rip_Indra (requires God's Chalice) – Pale Scarf + 300 fragments.

14. Cake Land

Level Range: 2100-2300

NPCs: Cake Guards (level 600-650)

Best Grinding Spot: Cake tower area – guards in large groups.

Hidden Chest: Top floor of cake tower – 20,000 Beli.

15. Frozen Dimension

Level Range: 2300-2550

NPCs: Frost Giants (level 650-700)

Best Grinding Spot: Ice field – frost giants in groups of 5.

Hidden Chest: Under giant ice block – 25,000 Beli.

Boss: Leviathan (sea event) – Leviathan Crown + 250 fragments.

Island Navigation Tips

  • Always carry a boat – you'll need it for sea travel between islands.
  • Use the teleport system once unlocked – it saves time.
  • Server hop if an island is too crowded – more players means slower spawns.
  • Mark important islands with waypoints to return quickly.

Pro Tips

  • Frozen Village (First Sea) and Floating Turtle (Second Sea) are the best grinding spots.
  • Great Tree and Cake Land are the most efficient for XP in Third Sea.
  • Always collect hidden chests – they add up to significant Beli over time.
  • Follow the level ranges – don't skip islands, you'll miss important NPCs and chests.
