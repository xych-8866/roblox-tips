Adopt Me Easter Eggs Guide 2026 – All Hidden Secrets & References

Adopt Me is full of Easter eggs and developer secrets. This guide reveals every hidden reference, secret interaction, and developer tribute hidden throughout the game.

1. Developer Grave – "Wolfpaq" Tribute

Location: Behind the church, in the small graveyard.

How to find: There's a gravestone with the name "Wolfpaq" – the original creator of Adopt Me. Click it once for a special message.

Reward: None, just a tribute.

2. Secret Arcade Machine – "Old Adopt Me"

Location: In the bowling alley, the farthest arcade machine on the right.

How to find: Click it 10 times – it plays a mini-game of the original Adopt Me map from 2017.

Reward: "OG Player" title after completing the mini-game.

3. Hidden Message – "Bethink"

Location: Inside the school, on the chalkboard.

How to find: Click the chalkboard 5 times – a hidden message appears: "Bethink – stay kind."

Reward: None, it's a developer message.

4. Secret Pet – The Rock

Location: Near the bridge (between the town and the playground).

How to find: Click the rock 50 times – it turns into a pet Rock. Yes, it's a real pet.

Reward: Rock pet.

5. Developer Office – Hidden Room

Location: Behind the nursery, there's a small door that's usually locked.

How to find: At 3 AM in-game time, the door unlocks. Inside is a mini version of the developers' office.

Reward: "Dev Visitor" title.

6. Secret Music – Piano in the Museum

Location: Inside the museum, there's a piano near the entrance.

How to find: Click the keys in order: C, E, G, C. The piano plays a secret melody – a tribute to the game's composer.

Reward: None, just hidden music.

7. Ghost NPC – Old Developer

Location: The cemetery at midnight (in-game time).

How to find: A ghost NPC appears at 12 AM. Click it – it says "Hello, player. Keep exploring."

Reward: "Ghost Seeker" title.

8. Rainbow Bridge – Secret Path

Location: Near the campsite, there's a rainbow-colored log over the river.

How to find: Walk across the log – it's a secret path to a small island with a chest (contains 50 bucks).

Reward: 50 bucks.
