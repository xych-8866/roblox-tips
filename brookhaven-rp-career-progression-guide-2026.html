Job Tiers Explained

Each job has 3 tiers. Higher tiers pay more but require more experience. You unlock the next tier by completing enough tasks in the previous tier.

Best Career Paths

Path 1: Police (Best for PvP players)

  1. Police Officer (~$1,500/hour) – 1-2 days
  2. Police Detective (~$2,500/hour) – 3-5 days
  3. Police Chief (~$5,000/hour) – 2 weeks+

Path 2: Pizza Delivery (Best for all players)

  1. Pizza Delivery – $4,000/hour from day 1
  2. No upgrade needed – it's already the best job

Path 3: Firefighter (Best for AFK players)

  1. Firefighter (~$500-1,000/hour) – low effort
  2. Fire Captain (~$1,500/hour) – 1 week
  3. Fire Chief (~$3,500/hour) – 1 month+

How to Level Up Jobs Fast

  • Police: Work with a partner to catch criminals faster.
  • Firefighter: Stay near the fire station to respond quickly.
  • Cashier: Play in busy servers – more customers.
  • Pizza Delivery: Use a fast car to reduce delivery time.

Pro Tips

  • Start with Pizza Delivery – it's the best job from day 1.
  • If you want PvP, go Police – it's the most rewarding career path.
  • If you AFK, go Firefighter – it's low effort and pays well at higher tiers.
  • Combine jobs – do Pizza Delivery for active cash, Firefighter for AFK cash.
