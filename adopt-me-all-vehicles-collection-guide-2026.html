Hidden Vehicles

  • Rocket Stroller: Swim under the pier at the beach – click the glowing item in the sand.
  • Mini UFO: Climb to the gas station roof – click the antenna 5 times.
  • Beach Buggy: Under the pier – click to claim.
  • Ice Cream Truck: Random spawn near playground – claim before it despawns.

Best Vehicles to Get First

  • Car ($10,000): Best value for everyday travel.
  • Rocket Stroller (free): Most valuable hidden vehicle.
  • Helicopter ($40,000): Best for fast travel across the map.
  • Event vehicles: Collect them during events – they rise in value.

Pro Tips

  • Save bucks for vehicles – they're useful and collectible.
  • Check event shop daily – limited vehicles appear sometimes.
  • Hidden vehicles are free – find them and save bucks.
  • Event vehicles are the most valuable – prioritize collecting them.
  • Use vehicles for faster travel – saves time during events and quests.
